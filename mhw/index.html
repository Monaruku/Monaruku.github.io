Getting your data out of the game

  1. Install REFramework (nightly) for Monster Hunter Wilds.
  2. Download the exporter script: mhw_stats_exporter.lua and place it in MonsterHunterWilds/reframework/autorun/.
  3. In-game, open the REFramework overlay and use Export stats (writes reframework/data/mhw-stats.json) or Copy to clipboard.
  4. Load the file or paste above. Bookmark your share link to keep the view.
