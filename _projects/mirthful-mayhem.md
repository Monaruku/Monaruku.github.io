## About

*Mirthful Mayhem: Comedy Quest* is a browser-playable 2D top-down shooter created during **Global Game Jam 2024** by team **Pixel Pioneers**. You play a crazy clown who blasts through waves of zombies in comedic, chaotic scenarios — with a dedicated "crazy mode" for when things get too serious.

## Highlights

- Built in a **48-hour game jam** window under the GGJ 2024 theme.
- Fully playable in the browser via **Unity WebGL** on itch.io.
- 2D top-down combat with a movement-first dash and a "crazy mode" release.
- Small team collaboration under the Pixel Pioneers banner.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD | Movement |
| Shift | Dash |
| Mouse + Left Click | Aim and fire |
| Space | Enter crazy mode |

Use the embed at the top of this page to launch the game in-browser, or open it directly on [itch.io](https://monaruku.itch.io/ggj-2024-mmcq) — no download required.
